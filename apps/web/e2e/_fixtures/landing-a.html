One operating context

Run the whole operation

Identity and access, governance, security, spend — a real product needs all of it, and the usual answer is a department for each. Golden Frijoles gives a maker and their agents one set of rails across the whole surface instead, so owning the product does not mean becoming five teams.

Agents with somewhere to work

More than an endpoint

Your agents can investigate, propose and act. Golden Frijoles keeps the context, permissions, staged changes and evidence visible, so autonomy can expand without the product becoming a black box. Governance and control over what your agents may do, without a department to enforce it.

Let agents move, keep the last word

Scoped credentials, staged actions, explicit approvals and an append-only record. The agent's work is something you can inspect, not something you have to trust.

The way of working behind the product

Learn it by making something real

Agents make it cheap to build something that demos well. What they do not give you is the taste to know whether it should exist, or the evidence that it held up once real people used it. The method came out of building Golden Frijoles, and the way to learn it is to use it: bring your own project, install the rails, design a Bet with your agents, build it, prove it, and find out what actually happened.
