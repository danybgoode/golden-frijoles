# Raw — funnel trend (after launch)

> If i launch a feature thats flagged and measured through tars, i should be able to measure its performance across
> time. This means that if i want to check how the features performed over the last year i should.
> — Daniel, 2026-10-12

`tars-one-model` makes a funnel read a feature's whole life (or the last 30 or 90 days). This seed is the trend: the
same stages by the month (or week) people first got the feature, so a founder sees whether adoption and return are
improving, as a line, not one number.
